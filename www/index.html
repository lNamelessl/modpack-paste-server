Connect to your server

  1. Open this service in Railway, then Settings, then Networking.
  2. Under TCP Proxy, enter 25565 and create the proxy.
  3. Railway shows a host and port. Add those in Minecraft under Multiplayer, Direct Connection.
  4. Install the exact same modpack version on your client, then join.

Your world, mods, configs and RCON password live on the volume at /data, so redeploys keep everything.

Changing the pack

Set MODPACK_URL to a different Modrinth pack page (or a CurseForge page plus CF_API_KEY) and Railway redeploys automatically. Existing worlds are kept.
